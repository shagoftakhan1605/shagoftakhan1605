## Hi, I'm Shagofta (Shaggy) 👋

I am a PhD researcher at the **University of Plymouth** (School of Biological and Marine Sciences). My PhD looks at how **thermal imaging** can be used for non-invasive, real-time welfare assessment in livestock, working with laying hens and dairy cattle.

My background is in computer science and data science (MSc Data Science, University of Sheffield). I enjoy turning biological and image data into tools that are useful and explainable.

### What I work with
- **Computer vision:** thermal and RGB imaging, object detection, segmentation, self-supervised learning
- **Machine learning:** PyTorch, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn
- **Data:** Python, NumPy, pandas, OpenCV, time-series analysis
- **Explainability:** Grad-CAM and visual model inspection
